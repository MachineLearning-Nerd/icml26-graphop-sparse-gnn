# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Ofek Amran, Tom Gilat, and Ron Levie for developing a graphop
framework that makes the dense-versus-sparse graph question precise enough to
study across probability spaces, fibers, approximation, and generalization.
The paper’s definitions and theorem anchors made it possible to preserve
verified structure, expose formal counterexamples, and distinguish proof-level
claims from finite MPNN evidence.

This repository is an independent reproduction and audit. It does not imply
review, approval, endorsement, or collaboration by the paper’s authors.
